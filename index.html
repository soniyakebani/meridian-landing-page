Before you order

Does the watch stop working if I cancel the Club plan?

No. Tide tables, depth, compass and logging are on the watch itself and stay there. You lose the shared dashboard and cloud history, and the last 90 days are exported to you automatically when a plan ends.

What happens if it floods?

Send it back and we replace it inside the three-year warranty, including the strap. We ask what depth and water temperature it happened at, because that's how we found the 2024 gasket issue.

Which coastlines are covered?

Roughly 9,000, weighted towards the North Atlantic, the Mediterranean and the Pacific Northwest. Send us where you swim and we'll confirm before you order rather than after.

Can I use it in a pool?

Yes. Set the length once and it counts laps, turns and rest automatically. It's the same sensor, just reading a much duller set of data.
